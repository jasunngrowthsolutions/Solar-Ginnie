An idea that started in Germany

Solar Ginnie is an industry-specific marketplace for renewable energy and other EPC products. We gather products from across the sector and sell them in one place.

Vision: to be the preferred renewable energy products provider in India.

Mission: to give customers the best experience across system integration: supply, installation, and operation and maintenance.

Founded by Syed Husain and his brothers.

  1. 2016

    Syed Husain visits Germany and sees renewable energy at work.

  2. 2019

    The idea takes shape.

  3. 2020

    The website goes live. Delivery partner integrations were not ready, which made shipping costly.

  4. Three years of research

    Operations paused while we studied the market and kept the brand alive.

  5. Relaunch

    Solar Ginnie returns with supply, installation and maintenance under one roof.

Government schemes that can lower your cost

Short guides on who qualifies and what each programme covers.

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

Launched 15 February 2024

A subsidy covering up to 40% of rooftop solar cost, aimed at 1 crore households.

You may qualify if you:

  • are an Indian citizen
  • own a house with a suitable roof
  • have a valid electricity connection
  • have not taken another solar subsidy

Decentralized Distributed Generation

28 solar plants totalling 548.4 kW power 3,765 households across 25 villages in 5 Uttar Pradesh districts, cutting about 660 tonnes of carbon a year.

Model Solar City: Ayodhya

Ayodhya is Uttar Pradesh's first model solar city. Lucknow, Varanasi, Agra, Noida and more are planned next.

Village Electrification

Remote villages get small hydro, biomass, wind or solar power, with 50–90% central assistance and five years of maintenance.

Micro Hydel and Wind programmes

UPNEDA leads small hydro projects up to 25 MW. Wind sites need about 4 m/s speeds to be feasible.

Rooftop solar in Uttar Pradesh

Grid-connected rooftop systems cut your bill and let you sell surplus power to the DISCOM.
